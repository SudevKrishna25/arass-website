# ARASS EVENTS — DATA PRIVACY & PARTICIPANT RETENTION POLICY

**Compliance Level**: GDPR / CCPA / ISO 27001 Data Minimization Principles

---

## 1. Data Collection Minimization Matrix

| Data Element | Purpose | Access Scope | Retention Period | Deletion Policy |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Participant Name & Email** | Authentication & Credential Delivery | System & Organizing Org | Active Account Duration | Deletable on user request |
| **College / University** | Academic Eligibility Verification | Event Organizers | Event Lifecycle + 1 Year | Anonymized after event closure |
| **Project Deliverables / Code** | Evaluation & Scoring | Allocated Jury Panel & Organizers | 3 Years (for portfolio/audit) | Squad IP retained |
| **Jury Scores & Feedback** | Meritorious Ranking & Calibration | Organizers & Evaluator Board | Permanent Audit Trail | Immutable Ledger |
| **Issued Certificates** | Public Credential Verification | Public (Verification ID only) | Permanent | Revocable, never destroyed |

---

## 2. Participant Account Deletion vs Legal Audit Trail
When a participant requests account deletion:
1. Personal contact fields (`email`, `phone`, `avatarUrl`) are anonymized (`anonymized_user_99@arass.local`).
2. Historical competition submissions and team member contributions are preserved to protect squad integrity and immutable score ledgers.
3. Cryptographically issued certificates of honor retain their SHA-256 verification hash.
